# Brag Plan: Voice Mail

## Planning rubric

1. An Android client for the existing carrier voicemail mailbox, with playback, search, and on-device transcription.
2. The useful claim: keep your chosen dialer and transcribe on your phone. README.md and RecordingActivity.java support both.
3. The visual hook is the actual mint-highlighted transcript beneath the media player.
4. Show native sample playback, phrase navigation, transcript search, and the three appearance modes.
5. 22 seconds gives the interaction time to read without repeating the previous 32-second edit.
6. Polished: a quiet product film with large type, close native UI crops, and purposeful transitions.
7. A low electronic bed with two restrained accents; no narration. Reuse the licensed current track, which needs no public credit.
8. I built an Android voicemail app with on-device transcripts and search. Here's a private-preview demo; design feedback is welcome.
9. Open a message, read the transcript, tap a phrase to hear that moment, then find a detail in its text.

## The angle and hook

Lead with the task: "Read it. Tap it. Hear it." A native recording is already visible in the first frame. Keep the same product panel on screen as the transcript moves into focus. The viewer sees the app work before the ending identifies the project.

## Key moments

- Real sample transcript and native playback position changes.
- Search highlights the word design in the saved transcript.
- The same native player and transcript in Light, Dark, and AMOLED.

## Outro

Voice Mail. Android / Mint preview. "Built for the messages you keep." This is a private development preview, not a public-store launch. Sample messages only.

## Tone, format, and visual identity

- Preset: polished, with app-store clarity.
- Format: vertical social, 1080x1350 (4:5), matching the user's LinkedIn direction.
- Duration: 22 seconds at 60 fps.
- Background: #101214; primary text: #F3F5F7; accent: #93E2C0; secondary: #AEB7C2, from Android night resources.
- Display/body: Roboto 700/400, the bundled face closest to native Android sans-serif. Native UI footage retains its own type.
- No device shell, rounded screen mask, camera cutout, decorative outline, floating loops, or architecture slide.
- Capture provenance: existing Generic rectangular S24-proportioned emulator footage; public sample callers and transcripts. No phone connection or new capture is necessary.

## Audio direction

- Current licensed electronic track: Coffee or Tea, Mixkit item 1168. Rights record: media/promo/MUSIC-LICENSE.md.
- Use a continuous 22-second excerpt from second 12, low level, short fade in and 1.2-second fade out. No loop needed.
- Music cue guidance: detect at composition time with Hyperframes beats; readability takes priority over exact beat locks.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle mint brand-mark presence from extracted RMS/bass data; no text scaling or visualizer.
- SFX: two quiet warm low-frequency accents, on the opening panel reveal and final brand landing. No click sounds that imply interactions absent from footage.
- No narration, artist-credit card, or public posting.
