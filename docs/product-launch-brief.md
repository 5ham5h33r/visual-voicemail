# Voice Mail launch film

## Audience and objective

A person scrolling LinkedIn should understand what Voice Mail does within the opening seconds. Show one useful task before explaining implementation. The film promotes the Android preview and asks for feedback; it does not announce a public store release.

## Direction

Use a flat charcoal canvas, the existing mint accent, and real UI content at readable size. Crop to the controls needed for the current task. Keep app cards at their native proportions. Use a rectangular generic emulator with the S24 Ultra's 384x832 dp viewport; exclude device bezels, camera cutouts, physical screen masks, and phone mockups.

The previous engineering edit showed too many small full-screen views, repeated helper text, and an architecture diagram. This version gives the viewer a shorter sequence of visible actions. Native playback and search establish the product's value; model choices add technical context after that proof. Light/Dark/AMOLED demonstrate fit without another settings walkthrough. In that sequence the canvas changes with the native theme, including true black for AMOLED, so the difference remains visible outside the cards.

The opening and ending identify the preview and sample messages. The film carries no music-credit card, artist line, version badge, or persistent engineering label. The licensed soundtrack permits this presentation. Keep rights records in development documentation.

## 32-second sequence

| Time | Message | Visual proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3.56 s | Your voicemail. Made clearer. | One actual sample message, introduced under the brand |
| 3.56-8.89 s | Go straight to the moment. | Phrase taps change native playback position; pause gives a readable hold |
| 8.89-12.44 s | Find the detail. | The same transcript rises into focus as the user searches for design |
| 12.44-17.78 s | Your mailbox. Your chosen dialer. | Native All/Unread/Saved navigation, with one complete message in view |
| 17.78-21.33 s | Transcribe on your phone. | Actual quality choices, model names, and download sizes |
| 21.33-28.44 s | Make it yours. | The same player/transcript content in Light, Dark, and AMOLED |
| 28.44-32 s | Voice Mail | Brand, Android/Mint context, development-preview status |

The cut grid follows four-beat bars at approximately 135 BPM. Native footage keeps its original timing. The title entrances use short ease-outs; the transcript focus move and theme changes use smooth easing. Typography is rasterized at double size, and fractional placement avoids whole-pixel snapping during slow moves. Transitions never invent app functionality. Saved sample text is scripted demonstration content, not recognition output from the muted public playback fixture.

## Evidence and review

The fresh generic emulator has no carrier session, contacts, account, or personal voicemail. The filming helper validates eight reserved-number sample messages and restores temporary metadata and preferences. Host screenshot streaming and NVENC avoid the slow guest encoder. Verify the active RTX 5070 Ti renderer, rectangular raw frame corners, capture timestamps, full video/audio decode, constant 60 fps output, and readable final search controls.

Use Apple's [app-preview planning guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-previews/) as a reference for a cohesive story, real UI, readable silent-viewing copy, and simple transitions. This LinkedIn film is an editorial product preview, not an App Store submission or an Apple/Samsung affiliation claim.
